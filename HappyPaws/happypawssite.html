A Simple Daily Routine for Your Dog

Dogs love routine. Knowing what happens and when makes them feel safe, and it helps reduce barking, chewing and anxiety. Here is an easy daily schedule you can start with:

  • Morning: Potty break, a 20-30 minute walk, then breakfast.
  • Midday: Fresh water, a short potty break and some playtime or training.
  • Evening: Another walk, dinner, and some time to play with the family.
  • Night: Last potty break and a quiet, comfortable place to sleep.

Puppies need more frequent meals and potty breaks, while older dogs may prefer gentler, shorter walks. Adjust the routine to your dog's age and breed.

Deworming Schedule for Puppies and Adult Dogs

Worms are very common in dogs, especially puppies, and many puppies are born with them. Regular deworming keeps your dog healthy and also protects your family. A general guide is below:

  • 2 to 8 weeks old: every 2 weeks.
  • 2 to 6 months old: once a month.
  • 6 months and above: every 3 months.

Signs of worms include a pot belly, weight loss, vomiting, loose stools and a dull coat. Always use medicine and doses recommended by your vet, because the dose depends on your dog's weight. This list is only a general guide.

Basic Feeding and Care Tips for Your Dog

A healthy dog starts with good food and regular care. Keep these basics in mind:

  • Feed a balanced dog food suited to your dog's age. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions and sweets, which are harmful to dogs.
  • Puppies eat 3-4 small meals a day. Adult dogs usually do well with 2 meals.
  • Always keep clean drinking water available.
  • Brush the coat regularly and give a bath once every few weeks.
  • Keep vaccinations up to date and visit the vet for regular checkups.

Most importantly, give your dog time, love and attention. A happy dog is a healthy dog!
